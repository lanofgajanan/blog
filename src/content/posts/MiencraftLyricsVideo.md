I recently saw a instagram reel showing a minecraft mod that shows lyrics of the song your currently playing in minecraft as text hovering around you.

I found it very cool and decided to make videos with this mod and some other mods

A heads up, all these mods are for fabric
and the minecraft version is 26.2

## Modlist
- the main mod is [show the lyrics](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/show-the-lyrics/files/9503824)
- i also found another mod, but its less customizable
still if you want to see it here it is, [Lyrics](https://modrinth.com/mod/lyrics)
- the modpack im using is called [fabulously optimized](https://modrinth.com/modpack/fabulously-optimized) Fabric version
- other mods include terrain generation mods like 
[Terralith](https://modrinth.com/datapack/terralith)

  [Tectonic](https://modrinth.com/datapack/tectonic)

  [Geophilic](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/geophilic)

- And the Legendry mod that allows insane render distances [Distant horizon](https://modrinth.com/mod/distanthorizons)

## Shaders
Im planning to use [Complementary Shaders - Unbound](https://modrinth.com/shader/complementary-unbound/versions)
and for the settings, im referring this video with some tweaks to world line setting [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gktj5B4Oig8&t=54s)
